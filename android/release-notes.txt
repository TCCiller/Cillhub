Cillhub 3.7.2 fixes a connection problem and closes two security gaps. If your account still has to accept the terms, the app now shows them and connects afterwards; before, it could not connect at all. A server can no longer make the app save a decrypted attachment outside its own cache folder, and other apps on the phone can no longer open app screens through crafted links. The app also no longer crashes on unexpected error replies from a server, and someone else reading a DM no longer clears your own unread mark or notification.
